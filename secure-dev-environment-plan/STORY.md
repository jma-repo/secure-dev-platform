# STORY.md — Secure Development Environment and Permissioned RAG

## ① Intent Alignment

This deck is for a solution discussion held before the client's requirements are finalized. The audience is two kinds of people in the same company: IT and development leads who understand the technology, and managers who do not, but who own the budget and the risk. After the talk, we want them to believe three things: RAG is not training a model; "data stays inside the network" and "use cloud Copilot" are mutually exclusive choices; and security is not about buying a scanner, but about putting checks on every mandatory path. We also want them to make five decisions on the spot: whether data may leave the network, whether they have M365 licenses, which identity source to use, how many environments to split into, and the RPO/RTO and budget shape.

The deck has 15 slides, including three hero slides (cover, five security gates, closing). The visual tone is locked as "restrained, auditable, engineering-minded, no showing off": low-saturation deep green with one brick-red accent, and monospace type for numbers and file names. Content boundaries: must cover requirement clarification, the trade-offs among the three routes, offline feasibility and its cost, disaster recovery, and an honest boundary between verified and unverified items. Do not cover specific pricing, cloud architecture details that have not been implemented, or statements like "we have already finished everything."

## ② Page Skeleton

The deck has 15 slides in 3 chapters. The table of contents declares 3 chapters, so the deck has exactly 3 `type: section` divider slides, numbered 01/02/03 in sequence, with titles that match the table of contents word for word:
- Chapter 01 "Requirements Alignment" → divider P03, covers P03–P05
- Chapter 02 "Solution and Security Design" → divider P06, covers P06–P11
- Chapter 03 "Current State and Next Steps" → divider P12, covers P12–P14

The three hero slides (P01 cover, P09 five security gates, P15 closing) make up 20%, with at least one supporting slide between any two of them. Rhythm: P01 peak → P02 valley → P03 transition → P04/P05 valley → P06 transition → P07/P08 valley → P09 peak → P10/P11 valley → P12 transition → P13/P14 valley → P15 peak. There are 7 asymmetric-layout slides (P04/P05/P08/P09/P10/P11/P14; 47%, which is ≥ 40%). Symmetric layouts are used only for P07 and P13 (chart + insight), the section dividers P03/P06/P12, and the table of contents P02.
